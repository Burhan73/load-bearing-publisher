Load Bearing Publisher

A private publishing tool for one creator’s own YouTube channels.

Load Bearing Publisher is a small, self-hosted script that uploads short educational videos to the YouTube channels owned by its operator. It runs on a single personal computer on a daily schedule. It is not a product, not a service, and has no users other than its owner.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video file to a YouTube channel the operator owns.
  • Sets the title, description, tags and scheduled publish time for that upload.
  • Adds translated titles and descriptions so viewers see the video in their own language.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or process data belonging to anyone else.
  • It does not offer sign-in to third parties and has no other users.
  • It does not transmit YouTube data to any server other than Google’s own APIs.
